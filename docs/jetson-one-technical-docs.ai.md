## Caveats
- Figures conflict across sources: power 84 kW vs 88 kW; flight time 15 vs ~20 min depending on pilot weight and test.
- Prototype data (13.4 kW and 42.7 kg thrust per motor, 300 kg total thrust) does not necessarily apply to the production aircraft.
- A Zenodo paper (record 19573670) benchmarks the Jetson ONE but is an AI-generated speculative design study, not a Jetson document.
